# TATI brand implementation

Source: the logo and brand reference supplied in this chat. The logo is preserved unchanged at public/assets/tati-logo.png.

- Primary: #1048B4
- Secondary: #F59E0B
- Tertiary: #10B981
- Neutral: #FAF8F5
- Headings and body: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Labels and data: Space Grotesk

Darker blue, amber and green shades are used where text needs stronger contrast. Green supports progress and growth; blue leads navigation and primary actions; amber highlights key moments. Fonts load through the existing Google Fonts stylesheet, with system fallbacks.
